CORE-328PB microcontroller module

Altium Designer

An ATmega328PB with FT232RL USB-UART programming and auto-reset, and an LM1117 LDO with Schottky OR-ing between USB and external power. I replaced the castellated reset pad with an on-board reset pushbutton. Rev A is fabricated.
