Temperature-controlled DC fan board

Altium Designer, LTspice

Runs from a single 20–25 V DC input. Two synchronous buck converters make the 12 V and 5 V rails, a 3.3 V LDO feeds an STM32F411, and TMP36 sensing drives a PWM MOSFET fan driver. The LTspice simulation shows 18.5 mV of output ripple on the buck converter. Includes the BOM and a bring-up plan. Schematic and layout are complete; firmware is next.
