Dual-axis solar tracker

Project at NTI. Simulink and Altium Designer.

I modelled the full power chain in Simulink: PV panel, CN3791 charger, Li-ion protection and a 5 V boost stage, together with the tracking control. It runs as a model-in-the-loop simulation of a small tracker with a 6 W panel, a single 3 Ah Li-ion cell and a 5 V load.

I also designed the 2-layer PCB around the same chain, with an INA219 for monitoring and an ATmega328P as the controller.

Models, scripts and PCB files on GitHub
