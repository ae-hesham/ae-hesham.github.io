PSK Gate System

Race-timing gate for athletes at stadium tracks. I owned it end to end at ELKO EP.

The client's earlier system picked up IR interference at sunrise. Switching to a red-LED sensor removed it, and I designed the schematic and 2-layer PCB that runs the new setup.

Two Optex CDD-100N-R sensors per gate feed a logic stage that confirms each crossing. An optocoupler isolates the 12 V sensors from the 3.3 V Raspberry Pi logic.

The sensors and the handheld button connect over USB Type-C into one enclosure, so each one can be unplugged and replaced.

I took it from prototype through client validation, then assembled 12 units in 3 weeks. All 12 work, and the client has asked for further development.
